Frequently asked questions

Short, plain answers. Where something is not decided yet, we say so.

Is ILĀ taking orders?

No. This website is a prototype. Prices and stock are placeholders, and checkout does not place an order or take payment.

How do I find my size?

Use the size guide, which lists XS to XL in centimetres and inches. The chart is a dummy for now and will be replaced with ILĀ's real pattern measurements. If you are unsure, you can request a fitting or ask on WhatsApp.

Can I book a fitting?

The prototype includes a fitting request. The format (pop-up, studio visit or video call), the places and the times are to be confirmed by ILĀ.

Which payment methods will be offered?

Planned: cash on delivery, card, mobile wallet and InstaPay. None is functional in this prototype, and the final list is to be confirmed.

How much is delivery and how long does it take?

To be confirmed. ILĀ has not yet set delivery cost, delivery time or coverage. The first target cities are Cairo and Alexandria.

What is the returns policy?

To be confirmed. ILĀ has not yet set a returns window, conditions or an exchange process.

What are the garments made of?

Only one fabric has been confirmed so far: the Heavyweight Unisex Oversized Tee is 280gsm organic combed cotton jersey. Materials for the other pieces are to be confirmed.

Does this website store my data?

Your bag is kept in your own browser using local storage. The site sets no cookies, uses no analytics and sends nothing from the forms. See the privacy page.

More questions? Contact ILĀ.
